🎲 Stochastic Simulation Features:

This game uses Monte Carlo methods to simulate realistic survival probabilities:

  • Breeding success depends on energy levels and random chance
  • Egg hatching and gosling maturation have probabilistic outcomes
  • Predator attacks use distance-based probability calculations
  • Migration involves energy loss and weather variance
  • Random events (storms) can impact the entire flock
